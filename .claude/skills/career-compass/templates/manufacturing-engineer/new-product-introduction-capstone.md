# New Product Introduction Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Taking a new product design into production through a phased
   introduction
2. Reviewing the design for manufacturability and feeding changes back
3. Developing processes, tooling, and documentation together
4. Running pilot builds and qualifying the process with capability
5. Handing over to production with a validated documentation set

## What You'll Build
A complete new product introduction for a small multi-part product
(yours from the mechanical track, a peer's design, or an open-source
hardware product): a design-for-manufacturing review with implemented
changes, a process plan and line layout, tooling and fixtures, the
full documentation set (flow, PFMEA, control plan, work instructions),
a pilot build of at least ten units with first-article inspection and
capability data, a production readiness review, and a handover package.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse the methods and possibly the parts from earlier templates; the
  capstone is the phased introduction of a whole product.
- Ten pilot units is the floor for capability on at least one
  characteristic.
- The readiness review needs a "production manager" peer or mentor
  with authority to say no.

## Steps
1. Run the design-for-manufacturing review on every part and the
   assembly; log issues, propose changes, and implement agreed ones
   through change control.
2. Develop the process plan for every part and the assembly, the line
   or cell layout, and the takt-time target.
3. Design and build the tooling and fixtures needed, validating each.
4. Produce the documentation set: flows, PFMEAs, control plans, and
   work instructions with the traceability matrix.
5. Run the pilot build of ten units, with first-article inspection of
   the first and measurement of a critical characteristic on all.
6. Compute capability, investigate issues with root-cause methods, and
   implement corrective actions with a second short run if needed.
7. Hold the production readiness review against a checklist
   (documents, tooling, training, capability, supply), record the
   decision and open items.
8. Assemble the handover package and train the "operators," then write
   the introduction report with a retrospective.

## Extension Ideas
- Build a PPAP-style submission for a customer.
- Add a cost roll-up and compare to the target cost.
- Run a designed experiment on the least capable process.
- Build a production dashboard for the cell.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Phased new product introduction management
- Design-for-manufacturing feedback and change control
- Integrated process, tooling, and documentation development
- Pilot qualification and production readiness

## Industry Relevance

Consumer Electronics, Automotive, Medical Devices, Industrial Products. New product introduction engineers in these sectors own the path from design release to stable production, and the role is hired on exactly this lifecycle experience. A pilot-qualified product with a readiness review and handover is the definitive manufacturing engineering portfolio.
