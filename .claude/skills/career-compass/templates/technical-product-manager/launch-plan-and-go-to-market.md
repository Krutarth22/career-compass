# Feature Launch Plan and Go-to-Market

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Coordinating engineering, design, marketing, support, and sales toward
   a launch date
2. Staging the rollout with flags, cohorts, and go/no-go criteria
3. Preparing documentation, support, and messaging before launch
4. Monitoring launch metrics and guardrails and responding fast
5. Reviewing the launch and capturing lessons

## What You'll Build
A complete launch package for the feature you specified (executed for
real if you can ship it with a team or an open-source project, or as a
fully prepared plan otherwise): a launch checklist across functions, a
staged rollout plan with criteria, release notes and a help article,
positioning and messaging for the target user, an internal enablement
brief, a launch-day monitoring plan with guardrails, and a post-launch
review with metrics and lessons.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Shipping something real, even small, to real users is strongly
  preferred; an open-source release counts.
- If you cannot ship, the package must still be complete and reviewed
  by someone who has run launches.
- Messaging should be tested with two target users before finalizing.

## Steps
1. Write the launch brief: what is launching, for whom, why now, the
   success metrics, and the launch tiers (silent, soft, full).
2. Build the cross-functional checklist with owners and dates:
   engineering readiness, documentation, support training, marketing
   assets, sales enablement, legal or compliance.
3. Design the staged rollout: flags, cohorts, the go/no-go criteria
   at each stage, and the rollback procedure.
4. Write the release notes, the help article, and the in-product
   messaging, and test the positioning with two target users.
5. Write the internal enablement brief for support and sales: what it
   does, who it is for, common questions, known limitations.
6. Set up the launch-day monitoring: the dashboard, the guardrail
   thresholds, the alert routing, and the war-room plan.
7. Execute the launch (or a dry run with the team), logging decisions
   at each stage gate.
8. Run the post-launch review after two weeks: metrics versus targets,
   what went wrong, support volume, and lessons, and write it up.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a pricing and packaging decision for the feature.
- Run a beta program with feedback collection before full launch.
- Produce a short launch video or demo.
- Build a launch-readiness scorecard reusable across features.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Cross-functional launch coordination
- Staged rollout design with criteria and rollback
- Messaging, documentation, and enablement preparation
- Launch monitoring and post-launch review

## Industry Relevance

SaaS, Fintech, Consumer Platforms. Launch execution separates product managers who ship from those who only plan, and companies in these sectors ask candidates to walk through a launch they ran, including what broke. A complete package with a post-launch review is the evidence, and a real shipped feature makes it decisive.
