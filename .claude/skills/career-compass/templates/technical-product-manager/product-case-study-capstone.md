# End-to-End Product Case Study Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Owning a product outcome from problem discovery to measured impact
2. Working with engineers and designers through build and iteration
3. Making and documenting the hard calls: scope cuts, trade-offs,
   rollbacks
4. Measuring the outcome and reporting it honestly to leadership
5. Presenting the story as a case study

## What You'll Build
A complete product cycle on a real product with a real team (an
open-source project, a student or side project with collaborators, or
your workplace): research and problem statement, a specified and
prioritized feature, build coordination with a decision log, a launch,
an experiment or measured outcome against the success metrics, a
leadership-style outcome report, and a portfolio case study presented
to a product leader or mentor for critique.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse the artifacts from earlier templates on one continuous thread;
  the capstone is the outcome and the story.
- The team must include at least one engineer who is not you; product
  management is a collaborative role.
- Honesty about what did not work is graded higher than a polished
  success narrative.

## Steps
1. Choose the product and team, confirm the problem statement from your
   research, and align the team on the goal and metrics.
2. Finalize the specification with the team, prioritize it against
   their other work, and agree the plan and milestones.
3. Run the build: weekly check-ins, a decision log of every scope,
   design, and technical call, and the reasons.
4. Launch with a staged rollout and monitoring, logging the stage-gate
   decisions.
5. Measure the outcome against the success metrics with an experiment
   where possible, and analyze honestly.
6. Write the outcome report for leadership: the problem, what shipped,
   the result, the cost, what you learned, and what is next.
7. Build the case study: the narrative, the artifacts, the decisions,
   and the result, with the messy parts included.
8. Present the case study to a product leader or mentor, capture the
   critique, revise, and publish the portfolio version.

## Extension Ideas
- Run a second iteration on the feature from the outcome data.
- Write a public blog post on one hard decision from the cycle.
- Add a competitive and market analysis to the case study.
- Present the case study in a mock interview loop.

## Skills Demonstrated
- End-to-end product ownership with a real team
- Decision-making under constraints, documented
- Outcome measurement and honest reporting
- Case-study storytelling for product audiences

## Industry Relevance

SaaS, Consumer Technology, Platform Companies. Product management interviews in these sectors are built around case studies of products you owned, from problem to outcome, and candidates without a real cycle to describe struggle. A documented cycle with a real team, a measured result, and a critiqued case study is the portfolio those interviews reward.
