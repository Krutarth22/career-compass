# Recruitment, Consent, and Visit Management

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Planning recruitment with realistic funnels and sources
2. Conducting and documenting informed consent properly
3. Scheduling visits within protocol windows and tracking them
4. Collecting source data accurately at each visit
5. Retaining participants and handling deviations

## What You'll Build
A recruitment and visit management system for your study: a
recruitment plan with a funnel model and sources, screening logs, a
consent process with a documented mock consent discussion (recorded
with a peer as participant) and a consent checklist, a visit schedule
tracker that computes protocol windows, source document templates for
each visit, a retention plan, and a protocol deviation log with at
least three simulated deviations handled correctly.

## Student-Scope Notes
- The consent discussion must be practiced with a peer and evaluated
  against a checklist of required elements.
- The visit tracker can be a spreadsheet with window calculations;
  test it with edge cases.
- Simulated deviations should be realistic (a visit out of window, a
  missed procedure, a consent version issue).

## Steps
1. Write the recruitment plan: sources, expected funnel rates, the
   enrollment target and timeline, and the materials needed.
2. Build the screening log and pre-screening tools consistent with
   privacy rules.
3. Prepare the consent process: a script, a teach-back approach, and
   a documentation checklist; conduct a mock consent and have the peer
   evaluate it.
4. Build the visit tracker computing windows from the enrollment date
   and flagging upcoming and overdue visits.
5. Create source document templates for each visit capturing every
   protocol-required data point with attributable, legible,
   contemporaneous, original, accurate principles.
6. Simulate five participants through several visits, completing the
   source documents and tracker.
7. Introduce three deviations, document each in the deviation log with
   root cause, impact, corrective action, and reporting decisions.
8. Write the retention plan and a summary of the recruitment metrics
   and deviation handling.

## Extension Ideas
- Design and evaluate a recruitment advertisement through the ethics
  process.
- Build a participant reimbursement and stipend tracking process.
- Create a remote or decentralized visit procedure.
- Add a participant satisfaction survey and analyze it.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Recruitment planning and screening documentation
- Informed consent process conduct and documentation
- Visit scheduling within protocol windows and source documentation
- Deviation management and retention

## Industry Relevance

Clinical Research Sites, Academic Medical Centers, Hospital Research Departments, Site Networks. Recruitment and visit execution are the daily work of coordinators in these settings and the main drivers of a site's reputation with sponsors. A documented system with a practiced consent process and handled deviations shows operational competence.
