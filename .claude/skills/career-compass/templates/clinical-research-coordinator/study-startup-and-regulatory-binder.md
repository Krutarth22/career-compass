# Study Startup and Regulatory Binder

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Reviewing a protocol for feasibility at a site
2. Preparing and submitting an ethics application with consent documents
3. Assembling the essential documents required before enrollment
4. Training staff and delegating responsibilities
5. Passing a site initiation visit

## What You'll Build
A complete site startup package for a realistic protocol (a published
protocol adapted to a hypothetical site): a feasibility assessment,
an ethics submission package with a consent form written to readability
standards, a regulatory binder organized per the ICH essential
documents list with every document drafted or templated, a delegation
log and training records, a site initiation visit checklist, and a
mock initiation visit conducted with a peer acting as the monitor.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Complete free GCP training (many sponsors and institutions offer it)
  and include the certificate.
- Use publicly available templates from institutional review boards
  for the submission and consent documents.
- The mock visit must be conducted, with the "monitor" checking the
  binder against the checklist.

## Steps
1. Read the protocol and complete a feasibility assessment: population
   access, procedures, staff, equipment, timelines, and budget
   considerations.
2. Draft the ethics application and the informed consent form,
   checking reading level and required elements.
3. Draft or template every essential document for the regulatory
   binder: protocol and amendments, investigator brochure placeholder,
   approvals, curricula vitae, licenses, delegation log, training,
   laboratory certifications, and financial disclosures.
4. Organize the binder (physical or electronic) with a table of
   contents and a filing convention.
5. Prepare the delegation log with roles and the training records for
   each delegated task.
6. Build the site initiation visit checklist from sponsor and GCP
   expectations.
7. Conduct the mock initiation visit with a peer as monitor, log
   findings, and correct them.
8. Write a startup summary with the timeline, the binder index, and
   lessons learned.

## Extension Ideas
- Prepare a budget and a coverage analysis for the study.
- Draft a recruitment plan with materials for ethics approval.
- Build an electronic regulatory binder in a document system.
- Prepare a contract and budget negotiation summary.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Protocol feasibility assessment
- Ethics submission and consent document preparation
- Essential document management
- Site readiness for initiation

## Industry Relevance

Academic Medical Centers, Clinical Research Sites, Contract Research Organizations, Hospital Research Offices. Site startup is the first responsibility of coordinators in these settings, and a well-organized regulatory binder is what monitors and auditors judge first. A complete startup package with a mock initiation visit demonstrates readiness for the role.
