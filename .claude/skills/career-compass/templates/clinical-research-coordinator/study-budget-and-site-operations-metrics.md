# Study Budget, Coverage Analysis, and Site Metrics

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Building a study budget from the protocol's procedures and effort
2. Performing a coverage analysis of routine versus research costs
3. Negotiating with a sponsor from a justified position
4. Tracking site performance metrics across studies
5. Reporting to leadership on site operations

## What You'll Build
A financial and operational package for your study: a procedure-level
budget with staff effort, procedure costs, overhead, and per-visit
and per-participant totals, a coverage analysis assigning each
procedure to routine care or research with a rationale, a negotiation
memo responding to a sponsor's lower offer, an invoicing tracker, a
site metrics dashboard (enrollment rate, query rate, deviation rate,
visit compliance, time to activation) across your simulated study and
two hypothetical others, and an operations report to leadership.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Use published fee schedules or institutional rates where available
  and cite them.
- The sponsor's offer and the negotiation can be simulated with a
  peer.
- Metrics should be computed from your simulated study's real
  tracking data.

## Steps
1. List every protocol procedure by visit and estimate staff time per
   procedure and administrative task.
2. Build the budget with rates, procedure costs, overhead, startup and
   close-out fees, and conditional costs.
3. Perform the coverage analysis assigning procedures to routine or
   research with documented reasoning.
4. Receive a simulated sponsor offer below budget and write the
   negotiation memo with justification and acceptable positions.
5. Build the invoicing tracker tied to visit completion and
   milestones.
6. Compute site metrics from your study's data and construct two
   comparable hypothetical studies.
7. Build the metrics dashboard with definitions and trend views.
8. Write the operations report to leadership with the budget outcome,
   metrics, risks, and recommendations.

## Extension Ideas
- Build a site capacity model for taking on new studies.
- Add a study close-out financial reconciliation.
- Analyze cost per enrolled participant across recruitment sources.
- Prepare a feasibility questionnaire response with metrics.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Procedure-level clinical study budgeting
- Coverage analysis reasoning
- Sponsor negotiation preparation
- Site performance metrics and leadership reporting

## Industry Relevance

Academic Medical Centers, Site Networks, Hospital Research Offices, Independent Research Sites. Financial and operational management is what advances coordinators into research manager roles in these settings, and site leaders value staff who understand budgets and metrics. A budget with a coverage analysis and a metrics dashboard demonstrates that management readiness.
