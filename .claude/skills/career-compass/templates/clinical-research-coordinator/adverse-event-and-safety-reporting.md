# Adverse Event Assessment and Safety Reporting

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Identifying adverse events from visits and records
2. Documenting severity, seriousness, causality, and expectedness with
   the investigator
3. Reporting serious events to sponsors and ethics boards within
   timelines
4. Following events to resolution
5. Maintaining safety documentation for inspection

## What You'll Build
A safety management process for your study: an adverse event
identification and documentation procedure, a set of ten simulated
case scenarios worked through with assessments (severity, seriousness,
causality, expectedness) and investigator sign-off, serious adverse
event report forms completed for the serious cases with timeline
tracking, ethics board reporting decisions applied per policy, a
follow-up log to resolution, and a safety reporting summary.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Use published grading criteria (for example CTCAE) and cite the
  version.
- A peer or mentor plays the investigator for causality assessments.
- Timelines must be tracked against real regulatory requirements
  (twenty-four hours, seven days, fifteen days) with the source cited.

## Steps
1. Write the procedure for identifying and documenting adverse events
   at visits, from participant reports, and from medical records.
2. Prepare ten case scenarios ranging from mild expected events to
   serious unexpected events, including one death and one pregnancy.
3. For each case, document the event, grade severity, determine
   seriousness, assess causality with the investigator, and determine
   expectedness against the reference safety information.
4. Complete serious adverse event report forms for the serious cases
   with all required fields and narrative.
5. Apply the ethics board's reporting policy to each case and document
   the decision and, where required, the submission.
6. Track the reporting timelines from awareness to submission and
   flag any that would have been late.
7. Maintain the follow-up log until each event resolves or stabilizes,
   with follow-up reports where needed.
8. Write the safety summary and a process note on what was hardest to
   assess.

## Extension Ideas
- Build a safety reconciliation with a sponsor safety listing.
- Draft a safety training module for site staff.
- Add device-specific reporting for a medical device study.
- Prepare a Development Safety Update Report contribution.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Adverse event identification and documentation
- Severity, seriousness, causality, and expectedness assessment
- Timely serious adverse event reporting
- Safety follow-up and documentation

## Industry Relevance

Clinical Research Sites, Academic Medical Centers, Contract Research Organizations, Sponsors. Safety reporting is the highest-stakes responsibility a coordinator carries in these settings, and late or incorrect reports are the most common serious inspection findings. A worked set of cases with correct timelines demonstrates the judgment sponsors need.
