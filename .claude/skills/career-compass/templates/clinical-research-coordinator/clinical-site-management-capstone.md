# Clinical Site Management Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Running a study at a site from startup through close-out
2. Managing participants, data, safety, and documentation together
3. Handling amendments, monitoring, and an audit
4. Closing out the study and archiving
5. Reporting site performance and lessons

## What You'll Build
A full simulated study lifecycle at your site: startup with the ethics
submission and binder, recruitment and enrollment of simulated
participants through visits, data entry and query resolution, safety
reporting, a protocol amendment implemented with re-consent, two
monitoring visits with findings closed, a mock audit with responses,
close-out with document reconciliation and archival, and a final site
performance report.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse and extend the artifacts from every earlier template; the
  capstone is the continuous lifecycle.
- Peers play participants, the monitor, and the auditor.
- Keep a study diary throughout as the basis for the final report.

## Steps
1. Complete startup: feasibility, ethics submission, binder, training,
   and a mock initiation visit.
2. Recruit and enroll simulated participants, conduct consent, and run
   visits with source documentation and data entry.
3. Manage data queries and safety events as they arise, with correct
   documentation and reporting.
4. Implement a protocol amendment: ethics submission, re-consent where
   required, and updated procedures.
5. Host two monitoring visits, resolve findings, and document closure.
6. Undergo a mock audit against a published checklist and write the
   responses.
7. Close out the study: final visits, data lock support, document
   reconciliation, and archival with a retention schedule.
8. Write the final site performance report with metrics and lessons
   learned, and present it.

## Extension Ideas
- Run two studies in parallel and manage competing priorities.
- Add a decentralized element (remote visits, home nursing) and its
  documentation.
- Prepare a site quality management plan from the lessons learned.
- Mentor a new coordinator through the startup phase using your
  documentation.

## Skills Demonstrated
- End-to-end site-level study management
- Integrated participant, data, safety, and document handling
- Amendment, monitoring, and audit management
- Close-out and performance reporting

## Industry Relevance

Academic Medical Centers, Clinical Research Sites, Site Networks, Contract Research Organizations. Senior coordinator and research manager roles in these settings require having run a study through its entire lifecycle including audits, and interviews are built around that experience. A complete simulated lifecycle with an audit response is the definitive coordinator portfolio.
