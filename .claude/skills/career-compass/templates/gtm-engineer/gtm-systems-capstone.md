# Go-to-Market Systems Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Designing the end-to-end revenue system architecture
2. Integrating CRM, enrichment, outbound, product signals, and reporting
3. Operating it with monitoring, data quality checks, and change control
4. Measuring the business impact and reporting to revenue leadership
5. Documenting the system so operations staff can run it

## What You'll Build
An integrated go-to-market system for a realistic (or real, if you have
one) revenue team: the CRM model, enrichment and scoring, the guarded
outbound system, product signals, and the attribution and reporting
layer from earlier templates working together with a documented
architecture, monitoring and data-quality checks, a change-control
process, an impact report over at least four weeks, and an operations
manual.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse everything; the capstone is integration, operation, and
  measurement.
- If you have a real team to serve (a startup, a club, a small
  business), use them; otherwise run it as a simulated team with
  consenting participants.
- Four weeks of operation with weekly metrics is the floor.

## Steps
1. Draw the system architecture: every tool, every data flow, the
   identity keys, the schedules, and the owners.
2. Integrate the components so a lead flows from capture through
   enrichment, scoring, routing, outbound or nurture, product signals,
   and into reporting without manual steps.
3. Add monitoring: pipeline failures, enrichment coverage, alert
   volumes, and data-quality checks with a weekly health report.
4. Write the change-control process: how a scoring weight or routing
   rule changes, who approves, how it is tested and logged.
5. Operate the system for four weeks, publishing a weekly metrics note:
   lead volume, score distribution, routing outcomes, outbound
   performance, funnel conversion.
6. Make at least two data-driven changes through the change-control
   process and measure their effect.
7. Write the impact report for revenue leadership: what the system does,
   the metrics over the period, cost, and recommendations.
8. Write the operations manual for a non-engineer operator and have a
   peer follow it, then assemble the portfolio package.

## Extension Ideas
- Add territory and capacity planning automation.
- Add a self-serve experimentation framework for outbound variants.
- Add customer success workflows on the same signal layer.
- Present the impact report to a real revenue leader for critique.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Revenue systems architecture and integration
- Operating go-to-market pipelines with monitoring and change control
- Measuring and communicating business impact
- Operations documentation for non-engineers

## Industry Relevance

B2B SaaS, Fintech, Sales Technology Vendors. Go-to-market engineering is a young discipline in these sectors and hiring managers look for people who have run the whole system, not one automation, and who can show numbers and a manual an operations team used. An integrated system operated for a month is that evidence.
