# Operations Improvement Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Scoping an improvement project with a sponsor and a business case
2. Diagnosing with data, observation, and modeling
3. Designing an integrated solution across layout, staffing, inventory,
   and process
4. Piloting, measuring, and adjusting
5. Reporting results and handing over sustained practices

## What You'll Build
A complete operations improvement project for a real organization
(the one from your earlier templates, or a new partner): a project
charter with a sponsor and a business case, a diagnosis using time
study, flow analysis, and a validated simulation, an integrated
solution design evaluated in the model, a pilot implementation with
measured results against the baseline, a financial and service impact
summary, a sustainment plan with standard work and metrics, and a
final presentation to the sponsor.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse and extend the tools from earlier templates on one project.
- The sponsor must be a real person with authority over the operation.
- Measured pilot results, even modest, are required; modeled results
  alone are not enough.

## Steps
1. Write the charter with the sponsor: problem, scope, goals, metrics,
   timeline, and the business case.
2. Diagnose: collect data, observe, run time studies where needed, and
   build and validate a simulation of the current operation.
3. Identify root causes with the data and the model, and quantify the
   opportunity.
4. Design the solution across dimensions (layout, staffing, scheduling,
   inventory, process changes), and evaluate it in the simulation with
   confidence intervals.
5. Plan the pilot with the people involved, including training and
   risk mitigation.
6. Run the pilot, measure the metrics against the baseline, and adjust.
7. Compute the financial and service impact and compare with the
   simulation's prediction.
8. Build the sustainment plan, hand over to the operation, present to
   the sponsor, and write the project report with a retrospective.

## Extension Ideas
- Extend the solution to a second area and measure.
- Build a monitoring dashboard that the operation keeps using.
- Perform a formal cost-benefit analysis with sensitivity.
- Publish an anonymized case study.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Project chartering and business case development
- Data- and model-driven diagnosis
- Integrated operations solution design and pilot execution
- Impact measurement and sustainment

## Industry Relevance

Manufacturing, Logistics, Healthcare Operations, Consulting. Industrial engineers in these sectors are hired to deliver measurable operational improvement, and interviews revolve around a project with a sponsor, a diagnosis, and results. A capstone with a real sponsor and measured pilot results is exactly that story.
