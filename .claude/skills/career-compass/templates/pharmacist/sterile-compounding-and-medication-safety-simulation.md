# Sterile Compounding and Medication Safety Simulation

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Performing aseptic technique to compounding standards
2. Calculating and verifying compounded preparations
3. Verifying orders and preparations as the pharmacist
4. Analyzing compounding and verification errors
5. Designing safety systems for the compounding process

## What You'll Build
A compounding and verification package: aseptic technique practice
in a skills lab or a simulated hood with a documented process per
the applicable compounding standard, a calculation record for twenty
compounded preparations (dilutions, infusions, pediatric doses,
electrolyte additives) verified to one hundred percent, a pharmacist
verification simulation checking twenty prepared orders with seeded
errors and a detection rate, an analysis of three published
compounding error events with root causes, a safety system design
for one failure point, and a review by a pharmacist.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Aseptic technique practice requires a school or workplace lab; a
  simulated setup with sterile-technique practice materials is
  acceptable with the limits stated.
- Calculations must be perfect; track and remediate errors.
- Seeded errors in the verification simulation should reflect real
  error types.

## Steps
1. Study the applicable compounding standard and document the
   process steps, garbing, and environmental requirements.
2. Practice aseptic technique with the available setup and have a
   pharmacist or instructor observe and score.
3. Complete the twenty calculations with a second-check process and
   track accuracy.
4. Build the verification simulation: twenty orders and preparations
   with seeded errors (wrong drug, dose, diluent, label, expiration).
5. Perform the verification, record detections and misses, and
   analyze the misses.
6. Analyze three published compounding error events for root causes
   and contributing system factors.
7. Design a safety system for one failure point (an independent
   double check, barcode verification, a labeling standard) and
   describe its implementation.
8. Obtain pharmacist review and compile the package with a reflection
   on verification vigilance.

## Extension Ideas
- Add hazardous drug handling procedures.
- Practice non-sterile compounding with documentation.
- Build a compounding record template meeting standards.
- Analyze a real pharmacy's error reporting data if accessible.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Aseptic technique to standards
- Compounding calculations with verification
- Pharmacist order and preparation verification
- Error analysis and safety system design

## Credential Milestones
- Pharmacy students: compounding competency and calculations are
  assessed in laboratory courses; calculations are a major component
  of the licensing examination.
- Sterile compounding certifications and competency documentation are
  required by employers and regulators for practice in that area.
- Pharmacist verification responsibilities begin with licensure and
  continue with employer competency validation.

## Industry Relevance

Hospital Pharmacies, Infusion Centers, Compounding Pharmacies, Oncology Pharmacy Services. Compounding errors have caused serious patient harm and regulatory action, and pharmacists are legally responsible for verification. A package demonstrating technique, perfect calculations, verification performance, and safety design addresses the competencies these employers scrutinize most.
