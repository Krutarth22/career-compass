# Medication Therapy Management Case Series

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Collecting a complete medication history
2. Assessing each medication for indication, effectiveness, safety,
   and adherence
3. Identifying and prioritizing medication-related problems
4. Developing a care plan and communicating with the prescriber
5. Documenting and following up

## What You'll Build
A series of eight comprehensive medication reviews on simulated
patients with polypharmacy (an older adult with multiple chronic
conditions, a patient post-hospital discharge, a patient with renal
impairment, and others): for each, a complete medication list
reconciled from multiple sources, an assessment of every medication
against the four criteria, a prioritized list of medication-related
problems, a care plan with recommendations, a prescriber
communication in the standard format, a patient-facing medication
action plan, documentation in a structured note, and a review by a
pharmacist.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Use published case scenarios or construct realistic ones with
  reference to guidelines.
- Interaction and dosing checks must use a reputable database.
- Patient-facing materials must be at an appropriate reading level.

## Steps
1. Build eight patient cases with demographics, conditions,
   laboratory values, and medication lists from multiple sources
   with discrepancies.
2. Reconcile the medication list for each, documenting discrepancies
   and their resolution.
3. Assess each medication for indication, effectiveness, safety
   (interactions, dosing, adverse effects), and adherence.
4. Identify medication-related problems and prioritize by clinical
   significance.
5. Develop the care plan with specific recommendations, alternatives,
   and monitoring.
6. Write the prescriber communication using a structured format with
   the rationale and evidence.
7. Write the patient's medication action plan and personal medication
   list in plain language.
8. Document each review in a structured note, obtain pharmacist
   review, and compile the series with a reflection.

## Extension Ideas
- Conduct a real medication review under supervision at a clinic or
  community pharmacy.
- Add a deprescribing plan with a taper schedule.
- Add an adherence intervention with a follow-up plan.
- Deliver a mock patient consultation and record it.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Medication reconciliation from multiple sources
- Systematic medication assessment
- Medication-related problem prioritization and care planning
- Prescriber and patient communication

## Credential Milestones
- Pharmacy students: medication therapy management competencies are
  assessed in introductory and advanced practice experiences and map
  to the patient care process on the licensing examination.
- Practicing pharmacists: medication therapy management certification
  programs and provider status vary by jurisdiction.
- Board certification in pharmacotherapy or ambulatory care builds on
  this practice.

## Industry Relevance

Community Pharmacies, Ambulatory Care Clinics, Managed Care Organizations, Long-Term Care Pharmacy. Comprehensive medication reviews are a reimbursed clinical service and a growing part of pharmacist practice in these settings. A reviewed case series with prescriber and patient communications demonstrates the clinical reasoning that defines the modern pharmacist.
