# Formulary Drug Monograph and Journal Club

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Evaluating a new drug for formulary addition
2. Comparing efficacy, safety, and cost against alternatives
3. Presenting to a pharmacy and therapeutics committee
4. Leading a journal club on the pivotal trial
5. Recommending formulary status with restrictions

## What You'll Build
A formulary evaluation package for a recently approved drug: a drug
monograph in the standard format (pharmacology, pharmacokinetics,
clinical trials appraised, safety, dosing, cost analysis against
alternatives, and a recommendation with restriction criteria), a
journal club presentation on the pivotal trial with a critical
appraisal delivered to peers and recorded, a pharmacy and
therapeutics committee presentation with a recommendation, and a
review by a pharmacist.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Choose a drug approved within the past two years with published
  pivotal trials.
- Cost analysis can use published prices and cite them; state
  assumptions.
- Present to at least three peers for the journal club.

## Steps
1. Select the drug and gather the prescribing information, pivotal
   trials, and guideline positions.
2. Write the pharmacology, pharmacokinetics, and dosing sections from
   primary sources.
3. Appraise the pivotal trials for design, results, and applicability
   and write the clinical evidence section.
4. Summarize safety from trials and post-marketing sources.
5. Perform the cost analysis against therapeutic alternatives with a
   cost per course or per outcome.
6. Write the recommendation with formulary status and restriction or
   monitoring criteria.
7. Prepare and deliver the journal club presentation on the pivotal
   trial, recording it and collecting questions.
8. Prepare the committee presentation, deliver it to a peer panel,
   obtain pharmacist review, and compile the package.

## Extension Ideas
- Write a medication use evaluation criteria set for the drug.
- Add a budget impact model for a hospital.
- Prepare a therapeutic interchange protocol.
- Present at a pharmacy student research forum.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Drug monograph authoring to the standard format
- Clinical trial appraisal and safety evaluation
- Pharmacoeconomic comparison
- Committee and journal club presentation

## Credential Milestones
- Pharmacy students: formulary evaluation and journal club are
  assessed in coursework and advanced practice experiences, and
  literature evaluation appears on the licensing examination.
- Residency applicants: journal club and monograph experience is
  expected and discussed in interviews.
- Practicing pharmacists: formulary management is a core function of
  clinical and managed care pharmacy roles.

## Industry Relevance

Hospital Pharmacies, Managed Care and Pharmacy Benefit Organizations, Pharmaceutical Industry Medical Affairs, Health Systems. Formulary decisions shape medication use and cost across these organizations, and pharmacists with monograph and committee experience lead them. A complete package with a delivered committee presentation is a strong differentiator for clinical and managed care roles.
