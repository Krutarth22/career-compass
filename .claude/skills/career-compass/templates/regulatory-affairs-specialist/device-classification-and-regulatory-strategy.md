# Device Classification and Regulatory Strategy

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Determining a product's regulatory classification in target markets
2. Identifying the applicable pathway and predicate or reference devices
3. Mapping applicable standards and guidance
4. Estimating timeline and cost for the pathway
5. Presenting the strategy to leadership for a decision

## What You'll Build
A regulatory strategy for a realistic medical device (an existing
product you research, or a concept from the biomedical track): the
classification in the United States and the European Union with the
rules and codes cited, the pathway determination (510(k) with
predicates identified, De Novo, PMA, or CE marking under the MDR
route), a standards and guidance map, a regulatory timeline and cost
estimate, alternative strategies with trade-offs, and a strategy
presentation with a decision recommendation.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Use the agencies' public databases (classification, predicates,
  guidance) and cite every determination.
- Choose a product complex enough to require judgment (software as a
  device, a combination product, or a novel indication).
- Timelines and costs should be grounded in published data and
  labeled as estimates.

## Steps
1. Describe the device: intended use, indications, technology, and
   users, precisely enough to classify.
2. Determine the United States classification and product code via the
   database, and identify candidate predicates with a comparison table.
3. Determine the European Union class under the MDR rules with the
   rule cited, and the conformity assessment route.
4. Map applicable standards (general, particular, and software or
   biocompatibility as relevant) and guidance documents.
5. Build the regulatory timeline for the chosen pathway with
   milestones and dependencies, and estimate costs with sources.
6. Develop at least one alternative strategy (a different indication,
   a phased approach) with trade-offs.
7. Identify risks to the strategy (predicate weaknesses, evolving
   guidance, testing needs) with mitigations.
8. Write the strategy document and present it to a peer playing the
   executive team, recording their questions.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a third market (Canada, Japan, or Australia) to the strategy.
- Prepare a pre-submission meeting request package.
- Analyze a recent regulatory decision on a similar device.
- Add a reimbursement pathway consideration.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Device classification with cited rules
- Pathway determination and predicate analysis
- Standards and guidance mapping
- Strategy communication to leadership

## Industry Relevance

Medical Device Companies, Digital Health Startups, Regulatory Consulting, Contract Manufacturers. Regulatory strategy is the first question every device company asks in these sectors, and specialists who can classify correctly and lay out a credible pathway are essential from the start. A cited strategy document with alternatives demonstrates that judgment.
