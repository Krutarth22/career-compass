# Regulatory Submission Program Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Managing a product through strategy, submission, review, and
   post-market in two markets
2. Coordinating engineering, clinical, quality, and leadership inputs
3. Responding to regulator questions under deadlines
4. Maintaining compliance through changes
5. Reporting regulatory status to leadership

## What You'll Build
A complete regulatory program for your device across the United States
and the European Union: the strategy, the two submissions (or one
submission and one technical file) assembled from earlier work and
completed, a simulated review with additional-information requests
answered under a deadline, a change assessment for a design change
with a determination of whether a new submission is needed, the
post-market plan activated, a regulatory status dashboard, and a
leadership briefing on approval and ongoing obligations.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse everything from earlier templates; the capstone completes and
  connects them.
- Review questions should be drawn from published deficiency letters
  or a mentor's experience.
- Hypothetical data must be labeled everywhere.

## Steps
1. Finalize the strategy and the project plan with cross-functional
   inputs and milestones.
2. Complete the United States submission and the European Union
   technical documentation, with the clinical evaluation and risk
   file integrated.
3. Conduct the internal review and a mock notified body or agency
   review with a peer, producing deficiency lists.
4. Answer the additional-information requests within a simulated
   deadline, tracking the changes to the file.
5. Assess a design change: impact on the submission, whether a new
   filing or a letter to file is required, with documented rationale.
6. Activate the post-market surveillance plan and produce the first
   periodic report.
7. Build the regulatory status dashboard covering submissions,
   certificates, obligations, and deadlines.
8. Deliver the leadership briefing on approval status, conditions,
   ongoing obligations, and risks, and assemble the program archive.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a third market submission with a local representative plan.
- Prepare a unique device identification and registration package.
- Run a mock inspection of the regulatory function.
- Publish a regulatory intelligence summary of recent changes
  affecting the device.

## Skills Demonstrated
- End-to-end regulatory program management across markets
- Submission completion and review response
- Change assessment and compliance maintenance
- Regulatory reporting to leadership

## Industry Relevance

Medical Device Companies, Digital Health, Regulatory Consulting, Diagnostics. Regulatory affairs managers in these sectors own products across markets and through their lifecycles, and interviews ask for exactly that end-to-end experience. A complete two-market program with review responses and change control is the definitive regulatory portfolio.
