# Clinical Evaluation Report and Systematic Literature Review

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Planning a clinical evaluation with defined questions and criteria
2. Searching literature systematically and documenting the search
3. Appraising evidence quality and relevance
4. Synthesizing safety and performance evidence with a benefit-risk
   conclusion
5. Writing the report to regulatory expectations

## What You'll Build
A clinical evaluation for your device: a clinical evaluation plan
with the clinical questions, the state of the art, and appraisal
criteria, a documented systematic literature search across at least
two databases with a search log and flow diagram, an appraisal of
included studies with a defined method, a synthesis of safety and
performance evidence including post-market data sources, a benefit-
risk conclusion tied to the risk management file, and the clinical
evaluation report following the applicable guidance structure.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Use free databases (PubMed, a trials registry) and document every
  search string and date.
- Follow the current EU guidance structure and cite it.
- Appraisal must use a stated method applied consistently.

## Steps
1. Write the clinical evaluation plan: scope, the device and
   equivalents, clinical questions, state of the art, and appraisal
   criteria.
2. Design and execute the literature search with documented strings,
   filters, dates, and results per database.
3. Screen results by title, abstract, and full text with recorded
   reasons for exclusion and a flow diagram.
4. Appraise each included study for relevance and quality with the
   defined method and record the ratings.
5. Extract safety and performance data into evidence tables.
6. Review post-market and vigilance sources (public databases) for the
   device type and summarize.
7. Synthesize the evidence against the clinical questions and write
   the benefit-risk conclusion, cross-referencing the risk file.
8. Write the clinical evaluation report to the guidance structure with
   appendices for the search and appraisal.

## Extension Ideas
- Draft a post-market clinical follow-up plan from the gaps found.
- Prepare a summary of safety and clinical performance.
- Perform a meta-analysis of a performance endpoint if data permit.
- Update the evaluation with newly published evidence after a month.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Clinical evaluation planning
- Systematic literature search and screening
- Evidence appraisal and synthesis
- Benefit-risk conclusion writing

## Industry Relevance

Medical Device Companies, Regulatory Consulting, Notified Body Support, Digital Health. Clinical evaluation reports are mandatory and closely scrutinized in these sectors, and specialists who can run a defensible literature review are in short supply. A complete evaluation with a documented search and appraisal is a distinctive regulatory portfolio piece.
