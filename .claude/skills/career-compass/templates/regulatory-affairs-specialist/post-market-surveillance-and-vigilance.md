# Post-Market Surveillance and Vigilance Reporting

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Planning post-market surveillance for a device
2. Collecting and trending complaints and field data
3. Determining reportability of adverse events and filing reports
4. Feeding findings back into risk management and design
5. Producing periodic surveillance reports

## What You'll Build
A post-market system for your device: a surveillance plan with data
sources, indicators, and thresholds, a complaint handling process
with a simulated complaint set of at least thirty records, trending
with a statistical threshold and signal detection, reportability
decisions for each complaint against United States and European
Union criteria with decision trees, completed adverse event report
forms for the reportable cases with timeline tracking, a field action
assessment for one scenario, an update to the risk management file,
and a periodic safety update report.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Generate the complaint set with realistic variety (use errors,
  malfunctions, injuries, non-events).
- Use the agencies' public reporting forms and criteria and cite them.
- Timelines must be tracked against real requirements.

## Steps
1. Write the surveillance plan: sources (complaints, service, literature,
   registries, public databases), indicators, thresholds, and review
   cadence.
2. Build the complaint handling process and log, and enter the
   simulated complaints with investigation records.
3. Trend the complaints by type, product, and time, apply a
   statistical threshold, and document any signals.
4. Assess reportability for each complaint with the decision trees
   and record the rationale.
5. Complete the adverse event report forms for reportable cases and
   track timelines from awareness.
6. Assess one scenario for a field safety corrective action with a
   health hazard evaluation and a decision.
7. Update the risk management file with post-market data and any
   new hazards or changed estimates.
8. Write the periodic safety update report with the surveillance
   results and conclusions on benefit-risk.

## Extension Ideas
- Build a complaint trending dashboard with automated signal flags.
- Draft a customer notification letter for the field action.
- Add a registry data analysis to the surveillance.
- Simulate a competent authority inquiry and respond.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Post-market surveillance planning
- Complaint handling and trending
- Reportability determination and reporting
- Feedback into risk management and periodic reporting

## Industry Relevance

Medical Device Companies, Diagnostics, Digital Health, Regulatory Consulting. Post-market obligations have expanded under recent regulations in these sectors, and specialists with vigilance and surveillance experience are needed for every marketed product. A complete post-market package with reportability decisions demonstrates that expertise.
