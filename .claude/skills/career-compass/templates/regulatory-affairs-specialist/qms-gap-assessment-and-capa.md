# Quality System Gap Assessment and CAPA

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Assessing a quality management system against a standard
2. Documenting gaps with objective evidence
3. Opening corrective and preventive actions with root cause
4. Implementing and verifying effectiveness
5. Preparing for an audit

## What You'll Build
A gap assessment of a quality management system (a small company's
documented procedures if you have access, or a realistic set you
construct from templates) against ISO 13485 and the applicable device
regulation clause by clause, a gap report with evidence and risk
ratings, a corrective and preventive action process applied to the
top three gaps with root cause analysis, implementation plans, and
effectiveness checks, two written procedures to close gaps, and a
mock internal audit of one process with a report.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Constructing a procedure set from public templates is acceptable;
  seed it with realistic gaps.
- Cite the standard's clauses; do not reproduce the standard's text.
- The mock audit needs a peer as auditee.

## Steps
1. Assemble or obtain the procedure set and map it to the standard's
   clauses and the regulation's requirements.
2. Assess each clause: compliant, partial, or gap, with the objective
   evidence and a risk rating.
3. Write the gap report with a summary, the clause table, and a
   prioritized remediation plan.
4. Open corrective and preventive action records for the top three
   gaps with problem statements, containment, root cause analysis,
   and action plans.
5. Write two procedures (for example document control and CAPA itself)
   to close gaps, with forms and training records.
6. Implement the actions and define effectiveness checks with criteria
   and dates.
7. Conduct the mock internal audit of one process with an audit plan,
   checklist, findings, and a report.
8. Close the corrective actions with effectiveness evidence and write
   the management review input summary.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a supplier qualification and control process.
- Prepare a complaint handling and vigilance reporting procedure.
- Map the system to a second regulation and identify deltas.
- Run a management review meeting with minutes.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Clause-by-clause quality system assessment
- Gap reporting with evidence and prioritization
- Corrective and preventive action management
- Internal auditing and procedure authoring

## Industry Relevance

Medical Device Companies, Contract Manufacturers, Regulatory and Quality Consulting, Diagnostics. Quality system competence is inseparable from regulatory affairs in these sectors, and many specialist roles are combined quality and regulatory positions. A gap assessment with closed corrective actions and an audit demonstrates the quality half of the role.
