# Site Grading and Stormwater Design

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Gathering existing conditions from survey and GIS data
2. Designing grading for drainage, accessibility, and earthwork balance
3. Sizing stormwater conveyance and detention to local criteria
4. Producing grading and drainage plans and a drainage report
5. Coordinating with the site's other disciplines and the reviewer

## What You'll Build
A site design for a small development parcel (a small commercial lot or
a community building site, real or from public GIS data): existing
conditions from public topography, a grading plan with proposed
contours and spot elevations that drains, an earthwork estimate, a
hydrologic analysis for pre- and post-development runoff, a sized storm
sewer and detention facility meeting a stated criterion, plan sheets,
and a drainage report.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Public LiDAR or GIS topography is sufficient for existing conditions;
  document its source and limits.
- Free or student civil CAD (Civil 3D student, QGIS plus a CAD tool) is
  enough; hydrology can be done in a spreadsheet or free software.
- Adopt a real jurisdiction's stormwater criteria and cite them.

## Steps
1. Select the parcel and gather existing conditions: topography, soils,
   property lines, existing drainage, and utilities from public sources.
2. Lay out the site program (building pad, parking, access) and
   establish design constraints: accessibility slopes, setbacks, and
   discharge points.
3. Design the grading: proposed contours, spot elevations at critical
   points, swales, and pad elevations, checking positive drainage
   everywhere.
4. Compute earthwork cut and fill and adjust the grading toward balance.
5. Run the hydrology: delineate drainage areas, compute pre- and
   post-development peak flows and volumes for the required storms.
6. Size the conveyance (inlets, pipes) and a detention facility with an
   outlet structure that meets the release-rate criterion, with a
   routing calculation.
7. Produce the plan sheets: existing conditions, grading, drainage, and
   details, with a title block and notes.
8. Write the drainage report with methodology, calculations, results
   against criteria, and assumptions; have a peer review it as a
   plan-checker would.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a water quality treatment facility and its sizing.
- Design an erosion and sediment control plan for construction.
- Add a utility plan with water and sewer connections.
- Model the site in a hydraulic package and compare to hand routing.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Existing conditions assembly from survey and GIS data
- Grading design with drainage and earthwork balance
- Hydrologic and hydraulic analysis and detention design
- Civil plan production and drainage reporting

## Industry Relevance

Land Development Consulting, Municipal Engineering, Infrastructure Design Firms. Site civil work is the largest employer of civil engineers in these sectors, and grading and drainage plans with a report are the core deliverables. A complete set on a real parcel to a real jurisdiction's criteria mirrors the job precisely.
