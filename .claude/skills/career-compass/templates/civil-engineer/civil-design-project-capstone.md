# Civil Design Project Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Delivering a multi-discipline civil project through design phases
2. Coordinating structural, site, and construction considerations
3. Responding to a plan review with revisions
4. Producing a permit-ready style document set
5. Presenting the design to a client and a review board

## What You'll Build
A complete design for a small facility on a real site (a community
building, a small warehouse, a park pavilion with parking): schematic,
design development, and construction document phases with a record of
each, integrated structural design, site grading and stormwater,
utilities at a preliminary level, a coordinated BIM model and drawing
set, a specification outline, a cost estimate and schedule, a
simulated plan-review response, and a presentation.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse the analyses, models, and methods from earlier templates on
  one integrated project.
- A peer or mentor plays the plan reviewer and issues at least ten
  comments you must resolve.
- Clearly label the work as educational and unsealed.

## Steps
1. Write the project brief with the site, program, codes, and criteria,
   and produce the schematic design with alternatives and a selection.
2. Develop the structural design: loads, analysis, member design, and
   foundations, with a calculation package.
3. Develop the site design: grading, drainage, stormwater management,
   and preliminary utilities, with a drainage report.
4. Build the coordinated BIM model and produce the design development
   drawing set; hold a design review and log actions.
5. Produce the construction document set with details, notes, and an
   outline specification.
6. Prepare the cost estimate and construction schedule from the
   documents.
7. Receive the plan-review comments, respond to each in writing, and
   revise the documents with a revision issue.
8. Present the project to a mock client and review board, and assemble
   the portfolio package with the phase records.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a sustainability analysis (stormwater reuse, embodied carbon).
- Add a traffic and parking study for the site access.
- Prepare a permit application checklist for the jurisdiction.
- Perform a value-engineering pass with documented savings.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Phased, multi-discipline civil design delivery
- Integration of structural and site engineering
- Plan review response and revision control
- Client and review board communication

## Industry Relevance

Civil and Structural Consulting, Municipal Engineering, Design-Build Contractors. Firms in these sectors want engineers who understand how disciplines fit together through the phases of a real project, and the ability to respond to plan review is a specific mark of readiness. A phased project with a review response and presentation is the portfolio that demonstrates it.
