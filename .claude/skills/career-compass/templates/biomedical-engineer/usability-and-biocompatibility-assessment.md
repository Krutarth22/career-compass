# Usability Engineering and Biocompatibility Assessment

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Analyzing use scenarios and use-related risks
2. Running formative usability evaluations and refining the design
3. Planning and executing a summative usability test
4. Assessing biocompatibility and cleaning or sterilization needs
5. Documenting both for the design history and regulatory files

## What You'll Build
For your design-controls device: a usability engineering file per IEC
62366 with use specification, user interface characteristics, use
scenarios, use-related risk analysis, a formative evaluation with at
least three users and the design changes it drove, a summative test
protocol and report with critical tasks, and a biocompatibility
assessment per ISO 10993-1 with contact categorization, material
information, an evaluation plan, and a cleaning or sterilization
consideration, integrated into the risk management file.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Usability participants should match the intended users as closely
  as possible; document deviations.
- Biocompatibility testing itself is out of scope; the assessment and
  plan are the deliverable, using published material data.
- Cite the standards' current editions.

## Steps
1. Write the use specification and identify the user interface
   characteristics related to safety.
2. Define use scenarios, including hazard-related ones, and perform
   the use-related risk analysis linked to the risk file.
3. Run a formative evaluation with three users on early prototypes,
   record observations, and implement design changes.
4. Write the summative test protocol: critical tasks, participants,
   environment, data collection, and acceptance criteria.
5. Execute the summative test with at least five participants, analyze
   use errors, and write the report with residual risk conclusions.
6. Categorize the device's body contact type and duration and identify
   the biological endpoints to evaluate.
7. Gather material information and existing data, write the
   biocompatibility evaluation plan, and address cleaning or
   sterilization needs.
8. Integrate both files into the design history file and update the
   risk management file with the results.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a labeling comprehension study.
- Perform a toxicological risk assessment for one material.
- Design a cleaning validation protocol.
- Conduct a human factors analysis of an existing device's known
  errors.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Use-related risk analysis and usability testing
- Formative and summative evaluation design
- Biocompatibility assessment planning
- Integration with risk management and design controls

## Industry Relevance

Medical Device Companies, Human Factors Consulting, Combination Product Developers, Surgical Instrument Makers. Usability and biocompatibility files are required for every device submission in these sectors, and engineers who can produce them are needed on every project. Both files for a real prototype demonstrate readiness for device development roles.
