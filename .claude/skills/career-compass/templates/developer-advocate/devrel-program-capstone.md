# Developer Relations Program Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Setting developer relations goals tied to product outcomes
2. Planning content, community, and speaking as one program
3. Executing across channels on a calendar with measurement
4. Reporting impact with metrics product and marketing leaders accept
5. Feeding developer insight back into product decisions

## What You'll Build
A ten-week developer relations program for one product or open-source
project: a strategy with goals and metrics (awareness, activation,
community health, product feedback), a content calendar executed across
writing, video, and at least one talk, ongoing community participation
with feedback reports, a developer-experience improvement shipped or
submitted, a metrics dashboard, and a final impact report with a
retrospective.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse and extend the assets from earlier templates; the capstone is
  running them as a coherent program with goals.
- Choose metrics you can actually measure with the access you have and
  say what you would measure with more.
- Ten weeks with a weekly log is the floor.

## Steps
1. Write the strategy: audience, goals, the metrics for each goal with
   baselines, the channels, and the weekly time budget.
2. Build the ten-week calendar: publications, videos, community
   activities, the talk, and the developer-experience project, with
   dependencies.
3. Execute weeks one through five, logging output and metrics weekly,
   and publish the first feedback report to the product owners.
4. Ship or submit the developer-experience improvement (a quickstart
   rewrite, an SDK fix, a sample library) and measure its effect.
5. Deliver the talk and publish the recording and written version.
6. Execute weeks six through ten, adjusting the calendar based on the
   metrics from the first half, and publish the second feedback report.
7. Build the metrics dashboard covering every goal over the ten weeks.
8. Write the impact report: goals versus results, what worked by
   channel, product changes influenced, and recommendations, plus a
   retrospective, and assemble the portfolio.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a small ambassador or champions program.
- Run a hackathon or workshop and measure activation from it.
- Build an attribution model from content to signups.
- Present the impact report to the project's leadership for critique.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Developer relations strategy and goal setting
- Multi-channel program execution on a calendar
- Measurement and impact reporting
- Product influence through structured developer insight

## Industry Relevance

Developer Platforms, Cloud Providers, Open-Source Companies. Senior developer advocate and DevRel lead roles in these sectors require running a program with goals and reporting impact, not just producing content. A ten-week program with a dashboard and an impact report is the portfolio that matches those roles.
