# EHR Workflow Analysis, Optimization, and Training

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Observing clinical workflows and documenting current state
2. Identifying friction, workarounds, and safety risks in system use
3. Designing configuration or workflow changes with clinicians
4. Building training and support materials
5. Measuring adoption and satisfaction after change

## What You'll Build
A workflow optimization project on a real or sandbox clinical system
(an EHR training environment, an open-source EHR, or a clinic's
scheduling and documentation workflow you can observe): observation
notes and a current-state workflow map for one clinical process,
a friction and risk analysis, a redesigned workflow with configuration
changes in the sandbox, tip sheets and a training module, a simulated
training session with feedback, and an adoption measurement plan with
baseline data.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Open-source EHRs (OpenEMR, OpenMRS) provide free sandboxes; an
  observed real workflow with a clinic's permission is ideal.
- Observation must respect patient privacy; observe the clinician,
  not patient data.
- Training must be delivered to at least two people and evaluated.

## Steps
1. Select a clinical process (medication reconciliation, referral
   ordering, visit documentation) and observe at least three
   instances, timing steps and noting workarounds.
2. Draw the current-state workflow map with system touchpoints and
   pain points.
3. Analyze friction (clicks, duplicate entry, unclear fields) and
   safety risks, and prioritize.
4. Design the improved workflow and implement configuration changes
   in the sandbox (templates, order sets, defaults, layouts).
5. Build the tip sheet and a short training module with screenshots
   and scenarios.
6. Deliver training to at least two users, collect feedback, and
   revise materials.
7. Define adoption and satisfaction measures and collect baseline data
   (timing, clicks, survey).
8. Write the project report with the maps, changes, training
   materials, and the measurement plan.

## Extension Ideas
- Measure post-change timing and compare to baseline.
- Build a super-user program outline.
- Perform a usability evaluation with a standard instrument.
- Analyze system audit logs for workflow timing at scale.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Clinical workflow observation and mapping
- Friction and safety analysis of system use
- Configuration-based workflow redesign
- Training delivery and adoption measurement

## Industry Relevance

Health Systems, EHR Vendors, Consulting Firms, Ambulatory Networks. Analyst and application coordinator roles in these sectors center on workflow optimization and training, and clinician satisfaction with the system is a measured outcome. A project with observation, redesign, delivered training, and baseline measurement mirrors that job closely.
