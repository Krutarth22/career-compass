# Primary Care Quality Improvement Project

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Identifying a care gap in a primary care or clinic setting
2. Reviewing guidelines and evidence for the target measure
3. Implementing a workflow change with the team through PDSA cycles
4. Measuring with clinical quality metrics
5. Reporting and sustaining

## What You'll Build
A quality improvement project in a clinic where you work, volunteer,
or rotate (screening rates, chronic disease control measures,
vaccination uptake, medication reconciliation completeness): a
charter with an aim tied to a quality measure, a guideline summary,
a workflow map and cause analysis with the team, at least two PDSA
cycles with run charts, team engagement documented, a SQUIRE-format
report with a poster, and a presentation to the clinic's leadership
with a sustainment plan.

## Student-Scope Notes
- A clinic must agree to host; student-run and free clinics are
  common hosts.
- Determine whether quality project review or ethics exemption is
  required and document it.
- Two cycles with real measure data is the floor.

## Steps
1. Meet the clinic team, identify a measurable care gap, and write
   the charter with a SMART aim.
2. Summarize the guideline and evidence for the target measure for
   the team.
3. Map the workflow and analyze causes with the team's input.
4. Establish the baseline from clinic data over at least two weeks.
5. Run the first PDSA cycle with a change (a reminder, a standing
   order, a checklist) and study the run chart.
6. Adapt and run the second cycle, documenting team feedback.
7. Analyze results with balancing measures and write the report and
   poster.
8. Present to clinic leadership and hand over the sustainment plan.

## Extension Ideas
- Submit the poster to a primary care or physician assistant
  conference.
- Add a patient experience measure.
- Spread the change to a second clinic.
- Complete a quality improvement training certificate.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Quality improvement chartering on clinical measures
- Evidence translation to workflow
- PDSA cycles with clinic data
- Leadership reporting and sustainment

## Credential Milestones
- Physician assistant students: quality improvement and evidence-based
  practice are program competencies, and many programs require a
  capstone or scholarly project.
- Pre-program applicants: a completed project demonstrates leadership
  and systems understanding in applications and interviews.
- Practicing physician assistants: quality improvement participation
  supports certification maintenance and practice advancement.

## Industry Relevance

Primary Care Practices, Federally Qualified Health Centers, Hospitals, Physician Assistant Programs. Clinical quality measures determine reimbursement and accreditation in these settings, and physician assistants frequently lead improvement in primary care. A completed project with cycles and a leadership presentation demonstrates that leadership capacity.
