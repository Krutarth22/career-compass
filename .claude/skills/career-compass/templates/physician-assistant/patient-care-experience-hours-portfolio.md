# Patient Care Experience Hours Portfolio

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Obtaining a certification that permits hands-on patient care
2. Accumulating direct patient care hours in a paid or volunteer role
3. Documenting responsibilities in the categories applications use
4. Reflecting on the physician assistant role observed in practice
5. Building supervisor relationships for recommendations

## What You'll Build
A patient care experience portfolio: a certification enabling
hands-on care (emergency medical technician, medical assistant,
certified nursing assistant, or similar) with basic life support, a
role accumulating at least two hundred hours of direct patient care
with a documented log distinguishing patient care experience from
healthcare experience per application definitions, shadowing of at
least two physician assistants in different specialties with
reflections, a skills record with supervisor sign-offs, and a
supervisor evaluation.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Application services define categories precisely; log hours in
  those categories from the start.
- Competitive applicants often exceed one thousand hours; this
  template starts the record.
- Shadowing physician assistants specifically is important to show
  understanding of the profession.

## Steps
1. Choose and complete a certification pathway that leads to a
   hands-on role and obtain basic life support certification.
2. Secure the role and complete onboarding; confirm which duties count
   as direct patient care.
3. Log hours weekly with role, setting, duties, and supervisor, in the
   application service's categories.
4. Maintain a skills record and obtain sign-offs as you demonstrate
   each skill.
5. Arrange shadowing with at least two physician assistants in
   different specialties and write reflections on their scope,
   collaboration with physicians, and daily work.
6. Write three reflections on patient encounters addressing
   communication, ethics, and the team's function.
7. Ask supervisors about writing letters later and keep contacts.
8. Obtain a supervisor evaluation and summarize what the experience
   taught you about the physician assistant role.

## Extension Ideas
- Advance to a role with greater responsibility (paramedic, surgical
  technologist).
- Volunteer in an underserved clinic to broaden exposure.
- Interview physician assistants about their training experiences.
- Complete a medical terminology course.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Certified hands-on patient care
- Application-aligned experience documentation
- Understanding of the physician assistant role through shadowing
- Reflective practice and professionalism

## Credential Milestones
- Prerequisite coursework in progress (anatomy, physiology,
  microbiology, chemistry, biochemistry, statistics, psychology);
  verify each program's list.
- Patient care experience hours accumulating in the application
  service's categories; programs set minimums and competitive
  averages are much higher.
- Entrance examination (the GRE or a physician assistant-specific
  test) per program requirements.
- Application through the central application service with
  experiences, shadowing, and references documented.
- Requirements vary by program and country; confirm for your targets.

## Industry Relevance

Physician Assistant Programs, Hospitals, Emergency Medical Services, Clinics. Patient care experience hours are the single most scrutinized element of physician assistant applications, and programs distinguish sharply between hands-on care and observation. A portfolio with a certification, categorized hours, and physician assistant shadowing is the foundation of a competitive application.
