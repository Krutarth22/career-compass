# Physician Assistant Pathway Readiness Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Assembling a complete application or certification-readiness
   portfolio
2. Preparing for the certifying examination with measured practice
3. Practicing interviews and clinical scenarios with feedback
4. Mapping evidence to program and certification competencies
5. Planning licensure, first position, and continuing certification

## What You'll Build
A readiness portfolio for your next stage (program application or
certification and first position): application materials (personal
statement, experience descriptions in the service's categories,
references requested and tracked), an examination preparation plan
with baseline and progress data over at least eight weeks, a
competency evidence map linking your portfolio items (experience
hours, history and physical practice, therapeutics cases, rotation
log, quality improvement) to the competencies assessed, three mock
interviews or clinical scenarios with feedback, an ethics case
analysis, and a two-year milestone plan with contingencies.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Tailor to your stage; the structure holds for applicants and
  graduating students alike.
- Mock interviews should match the target's format (multiple mini
  interview or panel).
- Practice examination data must be real and tracked.

## Steps
1. Identify the next stage and its competency framework and build the
   evidence map from your portfolio.
2. Draft the personal statement and experience descriptions and obtain
   feedback from two reviewers including a physician assistant.
3. Request references with a packet for each and track them.
4. Take a baseline practice examination, build the study plan, and
   track progress weekly with remediation.
5. Complete three mock interviews or clinical scenarios with feedback
   and revise your approach.
6. Write an ethics case analysis on a scenario involving supervision,
   scope, or patient autonomy.
7. Assemble the portfolio with materials, exam data, interview
   feedback, and the ethics analysis.
8. Write the two-year milestone plan with decision points and
   contingencies, and review it with a mentor.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a specialty exploration analysis with shadowing in three
  fields.
- Prepare a teaching session for pre-physician assistant students.
- Build a longitudinal learning portfolio for certification
  maintenance.
- Present the portfolio to an advisor for a formal readiness review.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Application and portfolio assembly with competency mapping
- Measured certifying examination preparation
- Interview and scenario performance under feedback
- Stage planning with contingencies

## Credential Milestones
- Pre-program: prerequisites, patient care experience hours,
  entrance examination, central application, interviews, and
  acceptance.
- Program: didactic year with objective structured clinical
  examinations, clinical year with rotation logs and end-of-rotation
  examinations, and graduation from an accredited program.
- Certification: the national certifying examination (PANCE) after
  graduation, conferring the PA-C credential.
- Licensure and practice: state licensure, a supervising or
  collaborating physician arrangement per state law, prescriptive
  authority registration, and employer credentialing.
- Maintenance: continuing medical education requirements and periodic
  recertification (the PANRE or its longitudinal alternative).
- Requirements differ by country and state; confirm for your
  jurisdiction.

## Industry Relevance

Physician Assistant Programs, Hospitals, Clinics, Certification and Licensing Bodies. Admission and certification are the decisive gates of the physician assistant pathway, and both are won by demonstrated readiness with evidence. A complete readiness portfolio with a milestone plan is the definitive artifact for this pathway.
