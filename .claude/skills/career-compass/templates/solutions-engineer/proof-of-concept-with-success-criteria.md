# Proof of Concept with Agreed Success Criteria

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Converting discovery findings into a scoped, time-boxed evaluation plan
2. Agreeing success criteria in writing before any work starts
3. Integrating the product with the prospect's data or systems
4. Running the evaluation, tracking results against criteria daily
5. Presenting the results and the decision recommendation

## What You'll Build
A complete proof of concept for one of your discovery prospects (or a
realistic scenario built with a willing partner): a written evaluation
plan with success criteria, timeline, and roles, an integration of the
product with the prospect's sample data or a system through its API, a
results tracker updated during the evaluation, and a readout deck with
results against every criterion and a recommendation.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Use the same product as your demo and discovery templates so the
  proof of concept builds on real capability knowledge.
- Sample data can be anonymized real data from the partner or a
  realistic synthetic set they approve.
- Time-box to two weeks of part-time effort. Scope discipline is graded.

## Steps
1. From the discovery summary, draft the evaluation plan: objectives,
   three to five measurable success criteria with thresholds, data and
   access needed, timeline, and who does what on each side.
2. Review the plan with the prospect, negotiate criteria until both sides
   agree, and get written confirmation.
3. Set up the product instance for the prospect with their sample data
   loaded through the product's API or import path, scripting it so it
   is repeatable.
4. Build the integration the criteria require (a connector, a webhook, a
   report, an SSO configuration) with error handling and a short
   technical note.
5. Run the evaluation with the prospect's users, logging results against
   each criterion daily and every issue with its status.
6. Handle at least one scope-change request by referring to the agreed
   plan and either declining or trading scope explicitly.
7. Build the readout: results per criterion, issues and resolutions, what
   the production rollout would require, and a clear recommendation.
8. Present the readout, capture the decision and objections, and write a
   retrospective on criteria quality and scope control.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a value model estimating the business impact from the results.
- Add a production architecture proposal with sizing.
- Run a competitive comparison against an alternative on the same
  criteria.
- Turn the integration into a reusable connector with documentation.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Evaluation planning with negotiated success criteria
- Product integration through APIs and import paths
- Scope control during a customer evaluation
- Results communication and decision recommendations

## Industry Relevance

Enterprise SaaS, Data and AI Platforms, Infrastructure Vendors. Proofs of concept decide most enterprise deals in these sectors, and solutions engineers are judged on whether they run them to agreed criteria or let them sprawl. A documented evaluation with a readout against pre-agreed thresholds is the strongest work sample for the role.
