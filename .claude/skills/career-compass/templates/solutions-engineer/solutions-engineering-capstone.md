# Solutions Engineering Deal Cycle Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Running a full technical sales cycle: discovery, demo, evaluation,
   security review, proposal
2. Tailoring every artifact to one prospect's situation
3. Coordinating with sales, product, and security on a timeline
4. Managing objections, competitors, and scope across the cycle
5. Handing a won deal to implementation cleanly

## What You'll Build
A complete technical sales cycle for one prospect (a willing partner
organization playing a realistic buyer, or a real opportunity if you have
one): a discovery summary, a tailored demo built on your environment, a
proof of concept with agreed criteria and a readout, a security review
package, a solution proposal with architecture and rollout plan, an
objection and competitor log, and an implementation handoff document.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse the assets from earlier templates, adapted to this prospect.
  Tailoring is the work.
- The partner should include at least two people with different concerns
  (a technical evaluator and a business sponsor).
- Keep a cycle log from the first call to handoff, dated.

## Steps
1. Recruit the partner and agree on the scenario, then run discovery and
   produce the summary and qualification.
2. Tailor the demo environment and script to the prospect's situation,
   deliver it live to both stakeholders, and log every question.
3. Propose and negotiate the proof-of-concept plan, run it, and deliver
   the readout with a recommendation.
4. Answer the prospect's security and architecture questions with a
   tailored review package and a review presentation.
5. Write the solution proposal: the architecture for their environment,
   the rollout phases, the integration work, and the assumptions.
6. Maintain the objection and competitor log throughout, with how each
   was handled and its outcome.
7. Hold the decision meeting, capture the decision and reasons, and
   write the implementation handoff: commitments made, technical
   details, risks, contacts.
8. Write the cycle retrospective: timeline, what moved the decision,
   what you would change, and assemble the anonymized portfolio package.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a business value assessment with the sponsor's own numbers.
- Run the same cycle against a competing product to sharpen positioning.
- Present the retrospective to a sales team or mentor for critique.
- Build a reusable deal-cycle playbook from the artifacts.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Owning the technical side of a full sales cycle
- Tailoring demos, evaluations, and proposals to a real prospect
- Cross-functional coordination and objection management
- Clean handoff from sale to implementation

## Industry Relevance

Enterprise SaaS, Cloud and Data Platforms, Security Vendors. Solutions engineering interviews in these sectors ask for a deal you carried technically from discovery to close and what happened after, and a documented full cycle with a real partner, including the objections and the handoff, is precisely that story with evidence.
