# Mechanical Product Development Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Taking a product from requirements through concept, detail design,
   analysis, prototype, test, and release
2. Managing the design through gated reviews
3. Producing a complete release package suppliers and manufacturing use
4. Verifying the design against every requirement with evidence
5. Documenting for handoff and for a portfolio

## What You'll Build
A complete mechanical product of your own choosing (a folding stand, a
hand tool, a small enclosure with mechanisms, a fixture, a piece of
sports equipment) developed through concept selection, detail design,
analysis, DFM, prototype, verification testing, and release: gated
design reviews with records, a full drawing and BOM package, analysis
reports, a test report with every requirement verified, and a portfolio
case study.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse the methods from every earlier template; the capstone is the
  full process on one product.
- Twelve to twenty parts is a reasonable size. Scope for completeness
  over ambition.
- Hold at least three reviews (concept, detail, release) with someone
  who will challenge you.

## Steps
1. Write the product requirements and a concept study with at least
   three concepts scored against them; select one at a concept review.
2. Detail the design in CAD with design intent, run the structural and
   any thermal analyses with hand-calc validation, and produce the
   drawing package and BOM.
3. Apply design-for-manufacturing to every part with a chosen process
   and get quotes or DFM feedback; revise.
4. Hold the detail design review, log actions, and close them.
5. Build the prototype, write the verification test plan, and execute
   it with data for every requirement.
6. Run root-cause analysis on failures, implement corrective actions
   through engineering changes, and retest.
7. Hold the release review and issue the release package: drawings,
   BOM, analysis and test reports, and change history.
8. Write the portfolio case study with photos, the process, the
   decisions, the data, and a retrospective.

## Extension Ideas
- Run a small production batch and measure part-to-part variation.
- Add a cost-reduction pass with a target and a result.
- Perform a formal FMEA on the design.
- Build a second unit from the release package alone to test it.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Gated product development from requirements to release
- Integrated design, analysis, DFM, and verification
- Complete release documentation and change control
- Engineering communication and portfolio presentation

## Industry Relevance

Consumer Products, Medical Devices, Industrial Equipment, Automotive. Companies in these sectors run stage-gated development and want mechanical engineers who have carried a design through the whole process, and interviews focus on exactly such a project. A released product with reviews, analysis, and test evidence is the definitive mechanical portfolio piece.
