# HAZOP Study and Relief System Design

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Leading a structured hazard study on a set of P&IDs
2. Identifying causes, consequences, safeguards, and recommendations
3. Rating risk and applying layers of protection analysis
4. Designing overpressure protection and sizing relief devices
5. Tracking recommendations to closure

## What You'll Build
A HAZOP study on two nodes of your P&IDs with a team of at least two
peers, recorded in a standard worksheet with guidewords, causes,
consequences, safeguards, risk ranking, and recommendations, a layers
of protection analysis for the highest-risk scenario, a relief system
design for one vessel with a sized relief valve and documented relief
cases, and a recommendation tracking register.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Facilitate the HAZOP yourself with peers as the team; record it
  faithfully, including disagreements.
- Use a published risk matrix and cite it.
- Relief sizing follows API 520/521 or the equivalent; use a simple
  vapor or liquid case with sourced properties.

## Steps
1. Prepare: select two nodes, define design intent, gather P&IDs,
   operating conditions, and material hazards, and brief the team.
2. Facilitate the HAZOP session node by node with guidewords,
   recording every deviation's causes, consequences, safeguards, and
   the team's risk ranking.
3. Generate recommendations with owners and justify each against the
   risk matrix.
4. Perform a LOPA on the highest-risk scenario: initiating event
   frequency, independent protection layers with credits, and the gap
   to the target.
5. Identify the relief cases for a vessel (blocked outlet, external
   fire, control failure), and determine the governing case.
6. Size the relief valve for the governing case, select a device, and
   check inlet and outlet piping constraints.
7. Build the recommendation register with status and evidence of
   closure for at least half the items.
8. Write the HAZOP report and the relief design basis, and present the
   findings to the "plant management" (a mentor or peer).

## Extension Ideas
- Add a safety integrity level determination and a safety instrumented
  function design.
- Perform a dispersion or consequence estimate for a release.
- Extend the HAZOP to a startup or shutdown mode.
- Design the relief header and flare knockout at a preliminary level.

## Skills Demonstrated
- HAZOP facilitation and recording
- Risk ranking and layers of protection analysis
- Relief scenario identification and device sizing
- Recommendation management and safety communication

## Industry Relevance

Oil and Gas, Chemicals, Pharmaceuticals, Engineering Contractors. Process safety competence is a regulatory and hiring requirement in these sectors, and engineers who have facilitated a HAZOP and sized relief are treated as more senior. A recorded study with a LOPA and a relief design basis is a distinctive portfolio piece.
