# Process Design Package Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Producing a front-end engineering design package for a process unit
2. Integrating simulation, equipment sizing, control, and safety
3. Estimating capital and operating cost with a defined accuracy
4. Reviewing the design with a multidisciplinary team
5. Presenting the package for a decision

## What You'll Build
A front-end design package for your process: design basis, process
description, validated simulation, process flow diagrams with stream
tables, P&IDs, equipment list with sizing of major equipment (columns,
reactors, exchangers, pumps), utility summary, control philosophy,
HAZOP summary and relief basis, a class 4 capital and operating cost
estimate, an economic evaluation, a design review record, and a
presentation.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse and refine every earlier template's output; the capstone is
  the integrated package.
- Equipment sizing uses standard shortcut methods with cited
  correlations.
- Cost estimating uses published factors and indices; state the
  accuracy class.

## Steps
1. Finalize the design basis and process description, and update the
   simulation to the final design case.
2. Issue the process flow diagrams with stream tables and the utility
   summary from the simulation.
3. Size the major equipment: column diameter and height, reactor
   volume, exchanger areas, pump heads and powers, vessel dimensions,
   with calculations.
4. Issue the P&IDs and control philosophy, and update from the HAZOP
   recommendations.
5. Compile the safety summary: HAZOP findings, LOPA, relief basis, and
   the materials hazard summary.
6. Estimate capital cost from equipment costs and factors, and
   operating cost from utilities, raw materials, and labor; compute
   the economic indicators.
7. Hold a design review with peers across disciplines, log comments,
   and resolve them with a revision.
8. Assemble the package with a document index and present it as a
   decision-gate review.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a plot plan and preliminary piping layout.
- Perform a Monte Carlo economic risk analysis.
- Add a life-cycle emissions assessment.
- Develop a commissioning and startup plan.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Integrated process design package production
- Equipment sizing and utility summaries
- Cost estimation and economic evaluation
- Multidisciplinary design review and presentation

## Industry Relevance

Engineering and Construction Contractors, Chemicals, Energy, Pharmaceuticals. Front-end design packages are the product of process engineering groups in these sectors, and engineers who have assembled one understand how every deliverable connects. A complete package with a review record is the definitive process engineering portfolio.
