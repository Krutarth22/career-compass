# Medication Administration Safety Project

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Performing dosage calculations accurately under time pressure
2. Applying the rights of medication administration in simulation
3. Recognizing high-alert medications and interaction risks
4. Analyzing medication error cases with a systems lens
5. Designing safeguards and teaching them

## What You'll Build
A medication safety package: a dosage calculation practice record with
one hundred problems across oral, injectable, weight-based, and
infusion calculations with accuracy tracked to one hundred percent on
a final timed set, a simulated medication administration session
(a skills lab or a self-built simulation with a peer) recorded
against a rights checklist, a high-alert medication reference you
compile, an analysis of five published medication error cases with
contributing factors and system fixes, and a teaching module on one
safeguard delivered to peers.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Never practice with real medications outside a supervised lab; use
  simulation supplies.
- Calculation accuracy is non-negotiable; track and remediate every
  error type.
- Error case analyses use published reports from safety
  organizations.

## Steps
1. Complete the calculation problem sets by category, tracking errors
   and their causes, and remediate until a timed final set is perfect.
2. Build the rights of administration checklist and set up the
   simulation with orders, a medication administration record, and
   simulated medications.
3. Run the simulation with a peer as patient and another as observer
   scoring the checklist; record and debrief.
4. Compile a high-alert medication reference with the risks and
   safeguards for each class.
5. Analyze five published medication error cases: sequence, human
   factors, system factors, and the safeguards that would have helped.
6. Design one safeguard (a double-check protocol, a labeling
   practice, a communication script) and build a teaching module.
7. Deliver the module to peers and collect feedback.
8. Compile the package with the calculation record, simulation
   scoring, reference, case analyses, and the module.

## Extension Ideas
- Add an intravenous pump programming simulation.
- Perform a medication reconciliation on a simulated admission.
- Analyze a near-miss reporting system's data if available.
- Build a pediatric weight-based calculation quick reference.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Dosage calculation accuracy
- Safe medication administration practice
- Medication error analysis with a systems perspective
- Safety teaching

## Credential Milestones
- Nursing students: dosage calculation competency examinations are
  required before medication administration in clinical courses; most
  programs require a passing score at or near one hundred percent.
- Pharmacology and medication safety are heavily weighted on the
  NCLEX; the error analyses map to its safety and infection control
  content.
- Practicing nurses: annual medication safety competencies are
  required by employers.

## Industry Relevance

Hospitals, Nursing Schools, Long-Term Care, Ambulatory Infusion Centers. Medication errors are among the most common serious harms in healthcare, and nurses are the final safeguard at the bedside. A package demonstrating calculation mastery, simulated safe practice, and systems-level analysis addresses the competency employers and educators test most rigorously.
