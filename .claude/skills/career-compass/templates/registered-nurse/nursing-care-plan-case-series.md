# Nursing Care Plan Case Series

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Assessing a patient comprehensively using a nursing framework
2. Formulating nursing diagnoses with supporting data
3. Setting measurable outcomes and evidence-based interventions
4. Evaluating and revising the plan
5. Communicating the plan across the care team

## What You'll Build
A series of five complete nursing care plans on simulated or
de-identified case scenarios spanning medical-surgical, pediatric,
maternal, mental health, and community settings: for each, a
comprehensive assessment organized by a framework, prioritized nursing
diagnoses with defining characteristics, measurable patient outcomes,
interventions with rationales cited to evidence, an evaluation with a
revision, a concept map, and a documented handoff summary, reviewed by
a nurse educator or practicing nurse.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Use published case scenarios or textbook cases; de-identify anything
  from practice.
- Rationales must cite current evidence or guidelines, not only a
  textbook.
- A practicing nurse or educator should review at least two plans.

## Steps
1. Select five diverse case scenarios and read them for cues.
2. For each, complete the assessment using a framework (functional
   health patterns or a head-to-toe approach) and cluster the data.
3. Formulate and prioritize nursing diagnoses with the supporting
   defining characteristics and related factors.
4. Write measurable, time-bound outcomes for each priority diagnosis.
5. Select interventions with rationales cited to guidelines or
   evidence, including patient education and social determinants
   considerations.
6. Simulate evaluation data and revise the plan with reasoning.
7. Draw a concept map linking the patient's problems, data, and
   interventions, and write an SBAR handoff summary.
8. Obtain review from the nurse or educator, revise, and compile the
   series with a reflection on clinical judgment development.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a plan for a patient with multiple comorbidities and competing
  priorities.
- Convert one plan into a teaching plan for the patient and family.
- Add a discharge plan with community resources.
- Present one case at a student nursing meeting.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Comprehensive nursing assessment and data clustering
- Nursing diagnosis and outcome formulation
- Evidence-based interventions with rationales
- Care plan evaluation and team communication

## Credential Milestones
- Nursing students: care planning is assessed in every clinical course
  and underpins the clinical judgment model used by the NCLEX.
- Pre-nursing applicants: the series demonstrates readiness for
  program-level clinical reasoning.
- Progression toward clinical rotations requires current
  immunizations, background checks, and basic life support
  certification; verify program requirements.

## Industry Relevance

Hospitals, Nursing Schools, Long-Term Care, Community Health Agencies. Care planning is the structured core of nursing practice and the basis of the clinical judgment the licensing examination tests. A reviewed series across settings demonstrates the reasoning that nursing programs and preceptors look for.
