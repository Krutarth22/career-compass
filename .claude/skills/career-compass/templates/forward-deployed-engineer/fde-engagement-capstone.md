# Forward-Deployed Engagement Capstone

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Running a full engagement from discovery to measured outcome
2. Balancing customer asks against product direction and reusability
3. Delivering data, tooling, and model components as one solution
4. Managing timeline, risk, and stakeholder communication throughout
5. Feeding what the customer needed back to the product team

## What You'll Build
A second, larger engagement run end to end with a new customer or a new
problem at the same customer: discovery and scope, data integration, a
delivered tool with an LLM-assisted step if appropriate, deployment and
handoff, measured outcomes against the success metrics you set, a weekly
status record, and a product feedback memo identifying what should
become reusable product capability rather than bespoke work.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Reuse the patterns and code from the earlier templates; the capstone
  is about running the whole engagement with discipline and measuring
  the outcome.
- Six to eight weeks part-time is realistic. Scope to what you can
  measure by the end.
- The product feedback memo assumes you work for a vendor; if you do not,
  write it as if to the maintainers of the tools you used.

## Steps
1. Run discovery and produce a signed-off statement of work with baseline
   metrics, reusing your discovery guide and improving it from the last
   engagement.
2. Write the engagement plan: milestones, weekly cadence, risks, and the
   decision rights on both sides.
3. Deliver the data integration and validate it by reconciliation with
   the customer within the first third of the timeline.
4. Deliver the tool in iterations with user feedback rounds, adding the
   LLM-assisted step with an expert-labeled evaluation if the workflow
   warrants it.
5. Send a weekly status note every week: progress, risks, decisions
   needed, next steps. Keep every one.
6. Deploy into the customer's environment, complete the security
   checklist, and hand off with training and runbooks.
7. Measure the outcome against the baseline metrics after handoff and
   write the outcome report honestly, including what fell short.
8. Write the product feedback memo: the bespoke work that should be
   product features, the friction points in the tools, and evidence from
   the engagement. Assemble the portfolio package with anonymized
   artifacts.

## Extension Ideas
- Turn one bespoke component into a reusable module with documentation.
- Run a retrospective with the customer and publish the lessons.
- Build a reusable engagement playbook from your two engagements.
- Estimate the customer's return on the engagement and present it.

## Skills Demonstrated
- End-to-end engagement leadership from discovery to measured outcome
- Integrated delivery of data, tooling, and model components
- Stakeholder communication and risk management under a timeline
- Translating customer needs into product feedback

## Industry Relevance

Enterprise AI Vendors, Data Platforms, Consulting-Led Software Companies. Forward-deployed roles at companies in these sectors are defined by owning outcomes at a customer while feeding the product, and interviewers ask for a full engagement story with numbers and what you pushed back to product. Two documented engagements with measured outcomes are exactly that story.
