# Core Molecular Biology Techniques Portfolio

## Production Workflow Mirrored
1. Performing standard techniques with proper controls and records
2. Troubleshooting failed experiments systematically
3. Maintaining a lab notebook that another scientist can follow
4. Working safely with reagents and biological materials
5. Presenting results with appropriate quality metrics

## What You'll Build
A documented portfolio of core techniques performed in a lab you have
access to (a university teaching lab, a community biolab, a research
lab as a volunteer, or a well-equipped home lab for safe techniques):
DNA extraction with quantification, PCR with positive and negative
controls and gel electrophoresis, a restriction digest or a cloning
step, and either a western blot or a quantitative PCR, each with a
complete lab notebook entry, a troubleshooting record for at least
one failure, a safety assessment, and a results summary with quality
metrics.

## Student-Scope Notes
- Access to a lab is required; community biolabs and university
  courses are the most common routes.
- Work only with biosafety level 1 materials and follow the lab's
  safety training.
- The notebook is the deliverable as much as the results; follow
  ALCOA principles.

## Steps
1. Complete the lab's safety training and write a safety assessment
   for the techniques you will perform, including reagent hazards and
   waste disposal.
2. Perform DNA extraction, quantify by spectrophotometry or
   fluorometry, and record purity ratios.
3. Design PCR primers or use validated ones, run PCR with positive,
   negative, and no-template controls, and analyze by gel with a
   ladder.
4. Perform a restriction digest or a cloning step and verify the
   product.
5. Perform a western blot or quantitative PCR with appropriate
   controls, loading normalization, and replicates.
6. When an experiment fails (and one will), document the
   troubleshooting: hypotheses, the diagnostic you ran, and the
   resolution.
7. Keep a notebook entry for every experiment with the purpose,
   materials with lot numbers, procedure with deviations, raw data,
   analysis, and conclusion.
8. Compile the portfolio with annotated gel and blot images, quality
   metrics, the troubleshooting record, and a reflection on
   reproducibility.

## Extension Ideas
- Add a bacterial transformation and colony screening.
- Add a cell culture technique from the next template.
- Quantify the variability across technical replicates and discuss.
- Write a standard operating procedure for one technique.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Core molecular biology bench technique with controls
- Systematic troubleshooting
- Rigorous lab notebook keeping
- Lab safety practice

## Industry Relevance

Biotechnology, Pharmaceutical Research, Academic Labs, Diagnostics. Research associate hiring in these sectors is based on demonstrated hands-on competence with core techniques and evidence of good record-keeping. A portfolio with notebook entries, controls, and a troubleshooting record is what those interviews ask to see.
